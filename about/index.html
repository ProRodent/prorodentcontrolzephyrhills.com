The first call.

Ring (888) 686-4821 and a dispatcher asks four things. Where is the property, what did you see or hear, how long has it gone on, and do children or pets use the area? Your replies determine who is sent and what rides in the truck.

Nights, weekends and holidays all reach a person. Should nobody be able to arrive today, you are told on the call and given a believable window.

On-site routine.

  • Tour the roofline, then comb the attic, garage, crawl space and yard for new evidence.
  • Place traps or stations on the lanes the evidence marks.
  • Close openings using sheet metal, steel mesh and hard-cure sealant.
  • Come back to tally catches, test each seal and schedule cleanup where needed.

Sequence matters. Closing holes too early can lock an animal inside a wall, and scrubbing an attic while entries stay open guarantees scrubbing it again.
