Why timing counts in Florida.

Cool, clear nights can arrive suddenly. If the walls rustle the following morning, treat it as live activity and mention it when you call.

Fruit trees, feeders and trailers.

Fruit that drops and stays on the ground feeds rats, and a feeder with spilled seed does the same. Pick up fruit often, put a tray under the feeder and bring seed storage indoors. Those changes are cheap and often reduce traffic quickly.

A parked trailer, boat or golf cart can serve as shelter. Raise the hood or lift the cover now and then, and look for nest scraps and chewed wires before the next trip.
