Before you call, put the broom down

No phone call is required to follow these steps. They match what health agencies tell anyone who finds droppings.

  • A quick sweep feels harmless, yet it can launch particles that hang in still air for a long time.
  • Fresh air first, cleanup second: thirty minutes with the windows open is the CDC's starting point for closed spaces.
  • Skin contact with droppings, urine or nesting material is avoidable, and gloves make it so.
  • Cover the waste and its surroundings with disinfectant and let it rest before the first wipe.
  • Tie the bag, bin it and wash your hands once more.
  • Watch yourself for a few weeks, and call your doctor if flu-like symptoms or hard breathing appear.
