Combining services.

Typical jobs stack two or three services. The usual chain runs from inspection to trapping to sealing and then a follow-up trip. Cleanup and repair always trail behind, since fresh insulation is wasted if animals still use the space.

Because every stage carries a separate price, you can approve trapping and sealing today and postpone cleanup or repair until money allows.

Outside our lane.

No separate urgent page exists, because urgent calls reach the same line and get the earliest opening. Roofing, medical questions and structural repairs unrelated to rodents belong to other professionals, and we tell you so when they arise.

Questions about services.

Can I book only one service?

Our twelve pages deal with mice and rats. Should the property walk reveal another animal, we tell you and suggest who can help.
