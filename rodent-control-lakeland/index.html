Before you call: handling waste without raising dust

Agencies largely agree on the cleaning method, so the shared steps are summarized here.

  • Hurry less. Fast scrubbing motions raise exactly the dust you want to keep down.
  • Shut-up garages, sheds and attics hold stale air, so crack them open and step away for thirty minutes first.
  • Treat everything soiled as dirty until proven otherwise: gloves on, hands washed after, no exceptions.
  • Keep the liquid coming until nothing looks dry, then walk away for a few minutes.
  • Bag everything you collect before it travels anywhere, and knot the top firmly.
  • Persistent fever or shortness of breath after an exposure should be checked by a physician.
