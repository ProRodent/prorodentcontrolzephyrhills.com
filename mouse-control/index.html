What draws a mouse to your place.

Think of a mouse as a tenant with a short checklist. It wants shelter that stays dry, food within a few feet and a covered route linking the two. A pantry wall cavity ticks every box. So does a stack of plastic bins by the garage door, or loose insulation near the attic hatch.

Our climate stretches the season. Warmth and dampness linger for most of the year, so cold is not the only trigger. Heavy rain pushes animals up from low ground, and a cooled house stays appealing through scorching afternoons. Neither factor needs a messy or aging building.

Mice work nights, so most households only notice them once the house goes still. Listen at the pantry wall, in the water heater closet and along the garage wall that shares a side with the kitchen.

Evidence leads, traps follow.

Work starts at the freshest sign and spirals outward. Recent pellets, greasy smudges on baseboards and cereal boxes with chewed corners sketch a crude map, and every map like that ends at a hole. Traps placed blind snare a few mice while the opening stays wide.

  • Under kitchen and bath sinks, then behind the range and refrigerator, then in the water heater closet.
  • Garage: each door corner, the rubber strip underneath and the line where wall meets slab.
  • At the roof: the attic hatch, vent openings and anywhere a pipe or cable passes through.
  • Around the foundation: vents or skirting on whichever side stays damp.

Catch first, close second.

Snap traps or enclosed stations line the trail, parked where pets and kids cannot reach. Mice hug walls and probe anything new, so a trap pushed flush against the baseboard outperforms one in the open.

Sealing starts when early catches reveal which routes are live. Steel wool alone gets tugged out by determined teeth, so each gap receives flashing, mesh and sealant. Our exclusion page lists the metals we use.

Sensible habits for the wait.

Dry pellets should be left where they lie, and the broom stays put, because sweeping lifts dust. Before tidying, read the wet-cleaning steps on the CDC page below, and keep any bait outside the home.

Humidity makes storage choices count. Move pantry staples and pet kibble into lidded tubs, and lift the water bowl at night. None of that evicts mice, yet it takes away the prize that keeps drawing newcomers.

How you will know it worked.

A good result sounds like nothing at all: no fresh pellets and traps that stay empty. Badly fouled attic or wall voids call for attic and crawl space cleanup. Gnawed cable or insulation is rodent damage repair territory.

Before you call, do this and skip that

Moving calmly keeps fine grit from floating where you breathe.

  • Leave the broom in the closet. Bristles and vacuum exhaust both fling dry droppings into the air you breathe.
  • Shut-up garages, sheds and attics hold stale air, so crack them open and step away for thirty minutes first.
  • Hands are the main route between waste and your mouth, so gloves go on first and soap comes last.
  • Spray, pause, wipe: that is the rhythm, with roughly five minutes in the middle.
  • One tied-off bag is enough for towels and a carcass together, and a second bag helps if the plastic is thin.
  • If something feels wrong later, see a doctor and mention the cleanup so they can decide what matters.

Mouse questions we hear in Zephyrhills.

What is the smallest hole a mouse can use?

Imagine a pencil laid flat. Gaps that wide show up alongside drain pipes, behind dryer ducts, around door frames and where cable enters siding.

Are summers safe from mice here?

No. A cool house is as inviting in July as a warm one in January, and a week of storms can push mice off soaked ground. Gaps left over in spring often serve as the summer entrance.

Can I try hardware store bait while I wait?

Better to ask the technician first. Poisoned mice frequently die in wall voids, and the odor that results is a bigger headache than the mice were.

When will the noise stop?

It hinges on how many mice live inside and how many openings they use. We give a believable timeline after the walk-through and not before.

Is a call at 3 a.m. okay?

Yes. A person answers overnight, and we book a same-day trip if the schedule has room.
